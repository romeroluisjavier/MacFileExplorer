How to install on your Mac

No technical skills or terminal commands required. Just follow these 3 quick steps:

  1. 1

    Download the installer

    Click the blue button below. The installer file will land in your Downloads folder in just a few seconds.

    Download v2.5.0 (.dmg)
  2. 2

    Drag to Applications

    Double-click the downloaded file. In the window that pops up, drag the MacFileExplorer icon into your Applications folder.

    Applications
  3. 3

    Open for the first time

    Open your Applications folder, right-click MacFileExplorer, and choose Open. Click Open once more on the prompt — and you're done!

    Only needed the very first time. After that, open it normally like any other Mac app.

Did Apple show a security message when opening it?

That's completely normal for free open-source apps downloaded outside the App Store. If right-clicking doesn't show an Open button:

  1. Click OK on the macOS notice.
  2. Open System Settings (the gear icon) and go to Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway next to MacFileExplorer.

Want to use the Taskbar or AltTab? The app includes a simple guided wizard to grant permissions in under a minute.

Optional quick Terminal command (only if you prefer commands) 
xattr -cr /Applications/MacFileExplorer.app
